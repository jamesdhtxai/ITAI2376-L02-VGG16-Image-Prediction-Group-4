# Slide Deck Folder

The group slide deck should follow this structure:

1. Cover slide
2. Introduction slide
3. Two slides from each group member
4. Group conclusion
5. Individual contributions

## Two-Slide Pattern for Each Member

### Slide 1 – What I Expected vs. What VGG16 Saw
Include:
- your name
- the image you tested
- what you expected the model to recognize
- the original top predictions
- one or two sentences on what the result taught you

### Slide 2 – What Changed and Why It Matters
Include:
- rotation and noise results
- what changed compared with the original
- which change affected VGG16 more
- one or two sentences on what you learned about the tool

## Style

Keep the slides simple and learning-focused. The goal is not to explain code. Explain the **what and why** of using the tool, and define technical terms briefly when they are necessary.
